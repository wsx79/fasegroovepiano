## Next release

- **Chord colours: do these chords sound good together?** Colour every chord of the progression
  (slots and dots on the circle of fifths) by how well it sounds **with the chords around it**:
  the one before and the one after, and the progression as a whole.
  - **Bright, vivid colour:** chords that sound great together (shared notes, smooth voice
    leading, strong root moves such as down a fifth, ii–V–I, IV–iii–ii).
  - **Faded, dull colour:** chords that clash with their neighbours (few or no shared notes,
    semitone clashes, distant jumps on the circle, free chords that don't resolve).
  - Possibly also a colour on the line between two dots on the circle, showing how good that
    single move sounds.

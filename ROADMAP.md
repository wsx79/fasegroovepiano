Ideas planned for the next versions of **Fase Groove Piano**. When one ships, it moves to the
[CHANGELOG](CHANGELOG.md).

## Next release

- **Chord colours by harmony:** colour every chord (progression slots and circle of fifths)
  by how well it fits the key and the chord before it. Very harmonious chords (in the scale,
  close on the circle of fifths) get a bright, vivid colour; less harmonious ones (free chords
  outside the scale, distant moves) fade to a dull, grey colour. You see at a glance which chords
  are "safe" and which are bold.

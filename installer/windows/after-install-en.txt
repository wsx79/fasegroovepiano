Fase Groove Piano is installed!

VST3 PLUG-IN
- Open your DAW and rescan plug-ins if it doesn't find it automatically.
- Load "Fase Groove Piano" as an INSTRUMENT on a MIDI/instrument track.
- Installed in: C:\Program Files\Common Files\VST3\Fase Groove Piano.vst3

STANDALONE APP
- Start it from the Start menu. There's no DAW, so press PREVIEW or use
  Play mode -> Hold a key to hear the grooves.

FIRST STEPS
1. Load a progression (e.g. "Dorian Vamp i - IV") and a groove (e.g. "Soulful Push").
2. Press PREVIEW.
3. Drag "DRAG MIDI TO DAW" onto your favourite piano track.

User guide: https://github.com/wsx79/fasegroovepiano/blob/main/docs/GUIDE.md
